## Revision 2 (coordinator, before the code review): the rate VOID is applied
The selection reader (EmTableSelectionJob in blankfree_emtable_read.py) now applies the A2/A7/A8 rate VOID.
- Eligibility: a finisher is eligible iff the A8 emitted rate of its final table lies in [5.80, 14.49] Hz, a closed band.
  - The rate comes from the genmarg decode of the finisher's gated CV holdout: real units for real finishers, permuted units (shuffle 0) for the nulls. It is pooled over original frames and stored in extras.json.
  - A missing rate counts as VOID.
- Selection: the selected restart is the lowest S among the eligible real finishers of all arms.
- Nulls: the best null and the null spread are taken over the eligible nulls.
- Run-preserving null (report only): its best S is also taken among eligible finishers.
- The identity band is A7's definition and ignores eligibility.
- Stage-A ranking stays by S only; no decode is added.
- Outcomes when nothing is eligible:
  - no eligible real finisher: NO ELIGIBLE RESTART, and G4a.L2.2 reads CANNOT_TELL;
  - no eligible null: NO ELIGIBLE NULL, also CANNOT_TELL. This is my reading; the registered text names no outcome for it.
- The docstring states that A7's duration-freeze rerun does not apply here, because arms a and b are already durfrz.
- The report prints every finisher's rate and ELIGIBLE or VOID.
- Tests: new fixtures cover a VOID best real, a VOID best null, the closed band edges, all real VOID and all null VOID. The job's run() was exercised end to end on the SIGNAL case and on the NO ELIGIBLE RESTART case.
- Results: both test suites ALL PASS. The five other shims' job ids are unchanged (57/792/16/220/1). The em_table graph still has 37 jobs, 35 new.
- applied_restarts stays at 16. The coordinator ruled that the cap-based projection is the registered reading.

## Budget: the halving applied
Stage A projects above the 24 GPU-h bar, so the restart count halves to 16 per arm (seeds 1-16, shared across arms).
- Projection at the 100-iteration cap: 27.45 GPU-h per corpus with 32 restarts per arm.
- After halving: 13.7 GPU-h per corpus. The run-preserving null keeps its 8 seeds (6.9 GPU-h).
- Stage B: 158 s per iteration, so 0.88 h per 20-iteration chunk on a 4-GPU job.
- Worst-case allocation: about 107 GPU-h in total.
  - Stage A: about 35 GPU-h.
  - Stage B: about 70 GPU-h, over 10 chains of 2 chunks. The run-preserving null and rerun chains use only 2 of their 4 GPUs.
  - Reruns and diagnostics: about 2 GPU-h.
- Critical-path wall time: about 3.2 h plus queueing.

   - "the selected S minus its best S" is computed paired on both holdouts.
