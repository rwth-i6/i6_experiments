## J as implemented
The full definition is in the `unit_key` docstring.

**String rule:**
- y_t = k(z_t), then maximal runs of equal symbols are formed.
- Runs shorter than d_min = 2 take the symbol of the nearest preceding run of length >= 2 in the same utterance. At utterance start, they take the symbol of the nearest following such run.
- An utterance with no run of length >= 2 becomes one segment. No real utterance hits this case: the minimum length is 55 frames.
- Equal neighbours are then collapsed.
- Reported alongside: absorbed frames (frames in short runs) and relabeled frames.

**LM term:** the trigram of RtzbESkOedsT over the collapsed string. History starts at BOS, BOS; there is no EOS term; SIL is an ordinary symbol. This matches `prior.per_token_log_probs(order=3)` exactly, which the tests check.

**EMIS term:** log P(u | realised absorbed symbol).
- Train side: the MLE of the train-side table.
- Held-out: the train table add-alpha smoothed, with alpha = `blankfree_emtable.PSEUDO_COUNT` = 1e-3.

**DUR term:** A9's durinit law.
- Phone rows: max-entropy on [2, 25] with mean 4.413787.
- SIL row: uniform on [2, 50], built exactly as `build_prior_summary` builds it.
- Overlong segments (d > D_k) are scored in the duration term only, as ceil(d / D_k) near-equal chunks. The string is unchanged.

**Rate:** 50 x non-SIL segments / original frames, with band [5.80, 14.49] Hz.

**Destroyed corpus:** `utterance_permutation(tag, n, seed=em.NULL_CORPUS_SEED = 0)`.

## Undetermined by the spec (choices made; please confirm)
1. **Gold alignment.** The L2-0 fits used latent durations, with no frame alignment. I used the gilkeyio MFA alignment (the source of their gold strings) of the 2821 fit utterances, rasterised on the retained frames as MfaFrameLabelsJob does. The majority is over 40 labels, SIL included, with ties to the lowest id. The job fails if any unit is unseen.
2. **Overlong segments.** The spec covers only d_min. My rule splits the duration term into chunks and leaves the LM term alone. On the real subset, the held-out gold overlong fraction is 6 %.
3. **Held-out alpha.** It is not stated in the dispatch. I used 1e-3, the A11 precedent.
4. **Argmax key.** I read it literally as argmax_s m(u|s), with no unigram weighting; R4 used m·pi. m_phi.npz is saved so the other variant needs no re-run. The etas are all 28254 train.segments tags.
5. **Ladder draw.** The new symbol is uniform over the 39 non-gold symbols. Seeds are 1-5 via default_rng([pct, seed]); random keys use default_rng([0, seed]) with seeds 1-20.
6. **VOID keys in the verdict.** The verdict reads every listed key and names the VOID keys rather than removing them. On the subset, every destroyed-corpus key is VOID: rates of 1.1 to 1.5 Hz, from heavy absorption.

Changes:
- Overlong runs (decision 2). A run longer than D_k (25 frames for phones, 50 for SIL) becomes ceil(d/D_k) near-equal same-symbol tokens, with the longer tokens first. The tokens enter the trigram string (so the repeats are scored) and the duration term. Rate is now 50 × non-SIL tokens / frames. The absorbed and overlong fractions stay in the table; the overlong fractions are counted before the split. The delta takes its trigram context from the old tokens, and its windows end at the 3rd old segment.
- Argmax keys (decision 4). The primary key is k(u) = argmax_s m_phi(u|s)·pi(s). pi is the label-free prior occupancy: the trigram's unigram times the durinit mean duration (26 frames for SIL), normalised. The same pi is used for every phi. I used this fallback, not the 256-utterance E-step, because I measured the lattice E-step on the login node at 78.5 s for 604 frames (batch size 1) and 90.4 s for 1335 frames (batch size 2), with 4 threads. At that rate 256 utterances take about 3 to 5 h per phi. Each `PhiArgmaxKeyJob` also writes `key_likelihood.json` (argmax_s m) and `m_phi.npz` (m_phi, pi). The read shows these as `<name>_lik` in the group `argmax_likelihood`, report-only. The verdict uses the posterior keys only.
- The `KeyFloorReadJob` docstring now holds the verdict rule and choices 1 to 6. The module docstring's DECISIONS paragraph is printed in the read's rule header.

Routing: every stage-0 task is `mini_task` and runs on engine "short", which is the login-node LocalEngine, with gpu 0. They are not gpupack jobs and hold no Slurm node. There are 12 new jobs: gold (1 cpu), 10 argmax (2 cpu each) and the read (1 cpu), each with 6 GB and 0.5 h.

## Amendment 2 (2026-09-24, fitted pi and the conservative comparison; commit 6ae84c59)

This amendment supersedes the "pi" item and the SIL concern in Amendment 1. The commit is `6ae84c59`, made on the same branch with the same 4 paths staged explicitly. It is not pushed and not launched.

  - m_phi is held fixed. c(u) is the train-side unit frame count over the same `gp.TRAIN_SEGMENTS` as the etas, so it is label-free.
  - The fit is EM from uniform, run until max |change| < 1e-8, and asserts that the log-likelihood never decreases (`unit_key.fit_mixture_weights`, `unit_key.unit_frame_counts`).
  - `PhiArgmaxKeyJob` now takes `units` in place of prior_npz/duration_prior_json. The prior-occupancy function was removed.
