## Concerns
1. The null repair may be very slow or even infeasible. In the destroyed corpus, 1-frame runs are absorbed (d_min = 2), so the rate can only reach the band by making keys degenerate (a few dominant symbols). The bounded probe gained 0.19 Hz per 400 proposals. Extrapolating linearly gives about 7400 proposals per start, about 20 min at 8 threads, and more at 3 threads. The rule accepts only strictly-closer proposals, so the gain may shrink further. In-band null keys will therefore be structurally forced. The coordinator should decide before launch whether a hard band on the null is the intended comparison.
2. T0 randomises good starts: the argmax start fell from J -5.005 to -5.78 after sweep 1, with 391 units moved.
3. The null is reduced (16 random + 7 argmax starts) and disclosed in selection.json. The full null is estimated at about 900 CPU-h.

## Assumptions (spec undetermined)
- A repair proposal is accepted only if it is strictly closer to the band.
- The calibration pass uses the start key in the first sweep's order.
- Random class keys may be non-injective.
- Void is judged on the held-out rate.
- Thread counts are a resource choice.
- The best relabel map per key is chosen by train J.
