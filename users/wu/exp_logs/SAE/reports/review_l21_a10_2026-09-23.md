- The four readings:
  1. Uniform is excluded from "the largest K* among them". This follows A9's "reported only". It could
     differ from a literal reading only if K*(uniform) > both candidate K*s AND the candidates' mean-S

  3. No candidate in band at 48 gives NO WAVE SETTING. A10's fallback set would be empty; this gives no
     verdict in conflict with A10.
  4. An exact tie gives TIE. This has probability zero.
  None of the readings changes a verdict that A10 determines.

## 5. Launchability and efficiency (not blocking)
- Limits:
  - QOS normal: MaxJobsPU 512, MaxSubmitPU 8192, MaxWall 12 h.
  - The booster partition is OverSubscribe=EXCLUSIVE, so every job takes a whole node anyway.
  - The user currently has 10 jobs.
- Submission pattern: i6_core ReturnnTrainingJob.path_available (i6_core/returnn/training.py:338-362)
  releases each checkpoint as soon as the file exists, and RETURNN writes it atomically
  (torch/engine.py:1278-1298). The reads are therefore submitted during training, not all at once:
  about 13 jobs per 3.6 min across the six restarts. In the probe, the ep1 read was submitted at 18:08
  while training ran from 18:02 to 18:19.
- Queue waits: 34 min to 2 h 40 min per job in the probe (for example, 1973309 was submitted at
  18:18:37 and started at 20:58:04).
- Billing is higher than the build report says:
  - Mean Slurm elapsed over 86 forward jobs on 2026-09-23 is 100 s, not 35 s.
  - 650 forward jobs x 100 s whole-node = 18.1 node-h = about 72 GPU-h billed.
  - The restarts take 6 x 2.87 h = 17.2 node-h = about 69 GPU-h.
  - Total: about 141 GPU-h billed, not about 100.
- A cheaper equivalent grouping would be a batch job that runs the unchanged per-checkpoint forward
  configs (same epilog, callback and outputs) four at a time on the node's GPUs, one job per restart per
  pass. That cuts about 18 node-h to 2-3. It needs new code, tests and a review, and it moves the read
  ids.
- Merging the marginal and decode jobs is NOT equivalent, because the callback changes.
- The wave does not multiply this count. selection_reads reads only the final checkpoint: 44 forwards
  for 24 arms (16x2 + 4x2 + 2 + 2, asserted in test_graph).
- Conclusion: launch as is. Build the batch job only if another per-sub-epoch grid is planned.
