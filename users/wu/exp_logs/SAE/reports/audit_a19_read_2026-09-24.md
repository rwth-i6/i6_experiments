## 7. Notes on interpretation (no correction to the verdict)

1. **SAME LADDER is about the LIFT band, not the PER level.**
   - SAME LADDER means the same rho*_lift in A4's loose LIFT band (< 0.50). It does not mean the same PER.
   - Without k2, every rung is worse by +0.077 to +0.138 at ep8, and each interval is well clear of zero.
   - The trigram-only r30 and r50 arms get worse after ep1: 0.203 to 0.298 by ep4, then 0.268 at ep8. r70 wanders between 0.33 and 0.37.
   - So the lift is largely present at ep1, and the trigram alone lets it drift. With k2 the arms stay at 0.18 to 0.19.
   - Any report to the user should state both facts: "still lifts (LIFT band)" and "k2 is worth 0.08 to 0.14 PER at ep8".
2. **M comes from the k2 bed.**
   - M is the with-k2 seed spread (0.001 at ep8, 0.0002 at ep1). The trigram-only bed's own run-to-run spread is not measured.
   - The paired CI covers utterance and speaker sampling only, not training nondeterminism. The ep1 K2 HELPS rows at r30 and r50 (+0.017, +0.020) are the least robust labels for that reason. They are beside and never gating.
3. **The r100 labels carry no content.** Both r100 arms are content-free controls reading NO LIFT, so their TRIGRAM ENOUGH (ep1) and K2 HELPS (ep8) labels mean nothing about content.
4. **Gaps in the extraction report, now closed.** The extraction report left the rt source hashes MISSING and did not inspect the warn/error lines. I filled both from disk, in sections 2 and 5.
