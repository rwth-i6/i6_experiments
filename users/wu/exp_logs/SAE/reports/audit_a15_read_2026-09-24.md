## Specific checks
(a) Optimism of the emission map.
- The map uses only m_phi and m_gold. Both are averaged over the etas of the 500 scored utterances.
  Eta is a per-utterance acoustic mean/std projected through a PCA fitted on train-clean-100 (SpeakerEtaJob), so it carries no labels.
  No units, MFA labels or transcripts of the scored utterances enter the map. The gold phi
  (BlankfreeSupervisedReverseInitJob.16v7R6ztSq1u) was fitted on train units with seed gold phones, which is disjoint from D4.
  The only label channel is the disclosed gold phi. Computing the map on the same 500 utterances does not matter.
- r100 is not a valid null for a sharp phi. It is flat: its 40 symbols have nearest-gold-phone collapse (UNCL 37) and a 1-2 gap of 0.025 bits.
  A 40!-selection over near-identical rows has little room to gain, so its +0.044 R4 and +0.18 T3 bound only flat phis.
  The doc says T magnitudes scale with sharpness. So the claim that the EM T3 gains (+1.68 to +2.12) are "far outside that null" has no valid null.
  random_init is no stronger: it is also flat, and it maps every unit to SIL.
  A sharp destroyed-structure control was never registered (A15-E has only positive controls).
- Audit-side bound for R4 (a scratch computation, not a bankable number). Units were randomly partitioned into 40 groups,
  and each partition was scored with the best one-to-one group-to-phone map chosen FROM THE MFA LABELS, which upper-bounds any label-free map.
  Over 200 draws R4 = 0.118 mean and 0.129 max, against the EM phis' 0.29-0.31. The EM R4 is real structure, not selection. The R4 half of claim 2 survives.
- But the same bound procedure gives R4 = 0.287 for a partition that knows only 7 manner classes (V, stop, fricative/affricate, nasal, liquid, glide, SIL).
  A 13-class place/voicing partition gives 0.377. So an R4 of 0.29-0.31 does not separate phone-level from broad-class content.

(b) R4 correctness.
- In the code (r4_frame_accuracy, the battery's R4 block) the map is argmax_k m_phi(u | g(k)) pi(k), where pi is the bed prior unigram and g is the emission map (from JS only).
  MFA labels enter only the hit count. They do not choose the map.
- Baselines on these frames: majority class = SIL, 0.0800 (random_init's R4 is exactly this). Non-SIL majority = AH, 0.0760.
  Chance with the true marginals is 0.0388 (non-SIL 0.0383), and 1/40 = 0.025.
- The EM phis' SIL symbol is identity-mapped in all six (JS_sil direct = matched), so SIL frames lift both direct and matched R4.
  On non-SIL frames only: EM direct 0.029-0.082 (at chance or the majority level) and matched 0.256-0.285, against r70 0.310 matched and 0.340 direct.

(c) T3 primary under the emission map.
- It is the same-map contrast M(gold) - M(deranged): t3_contrasts applies inv to the gold string and to the donor's gold string alike (recomputed above). Nothing was dropped.
- It is not comparable across sharpness. The pooled absolute log p per frame (per_utterance.json) shows this.
  With the emission labelling, the EM phis give the gold string -6.03 to -6.74 and the deranged string -7.94 to -8.66.
  For comparison: r100 gives gold -5.26, r70 gives -4.82, random_init -6.54, and permphi's relabelled string -3.67 (gold phi -3.65).
  The EM T3 of about 2.0 comes from a harsh penalty on the wrong string, not from a good fit of the right one.
  The doc's caveat ("T ranks within one family, R4 across families") is right. The doc then breaks it twice: with the r100 T3 null,
  and by comparing T3-relabel magnitudes with r70 and r100 (only the sign transfers).

(d) Arithmetic: see "Re-derived numbers". The table matches everywhere. Range statements check out:
  12-50x (3.93/0.341 = 11.5, 3.93/0.078 = 50.4), R4 gain 0.17-0.22, T3 gain 1.68-2.12, relabel 1.22-1.70, claimed 21-29.

(e) Labels in selection: the only importers of phi_competence_battery / phi_emission_map are their two configs and two tests.
  No training or selection config consumes them. A16 (a) plans to use the emission-map relabelling. It must stay analysis-only:
  that map is chosen against a transcript-fitted phi, so using it to relabel a phi that trains or is selected puts gold into training.

(f) Alternative explanations that fit the numbers equally well.
- Broad-class partition with sub-splits. R4 matches the 7-manner-class oracle (0.287). The multiply-claimed phones (SIL, N, T, Z, W, AY, M)
  are close to one representative per manner class. The unclaimed phones hold 18.5-40.4 % of non-SIL frames:
  AO, AW, OY, UH 6/6; AE, G, JH, SH, TH, ZH 5/6; IH, EH 4/6; AH (the most frequent non-SIL phone, rank 1) 3/6.
  Against a pure broad-class reading: the gap between the nearest and second-nearest gold phone is 0.12-0.17 bits (r70 0.036),
  and only 38-50 % of a symbol's 2nd-3rd nearest phones share its nearest phone's manner class (the gold phi's own figure is 70 %).
  So the symbols are partly phone-specific and partly cross-class mixtures (for example, segment-contiguity clusters).
  The data do not decide between these readings.
- Poor generative fit rather than labelling. Even under the oracle-chosen relabelling, the EM phis fit gold strings worse than a content-free phi (see c).
