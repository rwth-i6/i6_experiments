and only to hardware or audio. A Tier-A miss is FAIL ("debugger before any rerun"). The phase file attributes the
miss to the i6 prior. That attribution is plausible but only asserted: nothing in the artifacts isolates it (see
"Attribution"). Under the gate's Verdict rule, P0 therefore closes only on a user decision, as it did for the
G0.R0 prior clause.

## Attribution of the miss: asserted, not evidenced

- The phase file (Cost screen) says the miss is "expected from the flatter prior" and also "confounded with the
  audio label". The debugger (`reports/debug_prior_ppl_2026-09-25.md` section 8) concludes: "the part owed to the
  prior is not isolated". Under a flat init the logits are exactly zero, but the step-1 lattice still carries the
  reverse-model scores on units and eta, and those come from the i6 audio.
- The deciding test T3 (one step with only prior_npz swapped) is specified there and was NOT run. No artifact runs
  i6 audio with a JUPITER-like prior.
- The G0.R1s line ("the +0.020 shift equals ctrl_20's, so it is attributed to the i6 prior") does not discriminate:
  ctrl_20_s1 shares both the i6 prior and the i6 audio.
- Blaming the audio would not rescue the clause either: the +-0.01 bracket is already the audio allowance.
