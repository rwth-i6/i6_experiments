## Method: why it is the same computation
The worker builds RETURNN exactly as `rnn.py` does. It calls `returnn.__main__.init_config` on the ctrl_20 `returnn.config`, then `init_log`, `init_backend_engine`, `init_engine` and `Engine.init_train_from_config`. The pinned RETURNN checkout is first on sys.path, and the worker asserts that this copy is the one imported.

Only four keys are overridden. `model` points at a directory of symlinks to the snapshot. `learning_rate_file` and `log` point into the run dir. `batch_size` and `max_seqs` are set to the shape under test.

The engine resumes at epoch 12 from epoch 11: it loads the model and the Adam state, starts at global step 627 with lr 1e-4, and the worker asserts `lattice_float64` is True.

The timed step is the body of `Engine.train_epoch` with accumulation 1, run on the engine's own methods, and it is bracketed by `torch.cuda.synchronize`:
- `zero_grad`
- `raw_dict_to_extern_data` (the host-to-device copy)
- `_run_step`, which runs the config's `train_step`
- `total_loss`, followed by the per-loss `.item()` reads
- `backward`
- `Updater.step`, which applies the global-norm clip at 5.0 and the Adam step with the param groups

I chose this over driving `engine.train()` for two reasons. RETURNN's own step duration is not CUDA-synchronised, and its DataLoader would impose the `laplace:.1000` ordering.

## Batches
- **Train split:** all 28,254 tags in `train.segments`, the `seq_list_filter_file` of the config's train data. The worker checks that every tag exists in the train HDFs.
- **Sampling:** a seeded permutation with `numpy default_rng(0)` gives a uniform draw without replacement.
- **Data path:** sequences are read through the config's own train MetaDataset, with the cache manager off (the same files are read in place). They are grouped by RETURNN's `BatchingIterDataPipe` and collated by `collate_batch`.
- **Padded-frame rule (pinned RETURNN, `pipeline.py:305-319`):** a sequence is added while `max_len * (n+1) <= N`. `max_seqs` closes a batch once it holds M sequences. Padded frames are therefore n_seqs times max_len, and the worker asserts this.
- **Per batch, the worker records:** n_seqs, real frames, max length, padded frames, the seq tags, the step time and l_tau.
- **Order:** 2 untimed warm-up batches come first, then K = 12 timed ones.

Seed-0 batches built on CPU:
- (a) 88000/128: 107–122 seqs per batch, 57.6k–66.0k real frames, max length 716–817, 87.2k–87.9k padded frames.
- (b) 44000/64: 53–62 seqs per batch, 28.8k–34.9k real frames, 40.2k–43.9k padded frames.
- For comparison, the training ran about 124 seqs per step.

## Driver
Each shape run is a fresh subprocess, so the allocator environment takes effect.
- (a) runs first with PYTORCH_CUDA_ALLOC_CONF unset. That matches the training: its log prints no such variable, and the config sets none.
- If (a) runs out of memory (worker exit code 3), the driver retries it once with `expandable_segments:True`, and both results are kept.
- (b) then runs with the default allocator.
- Outputs are `<run_tag>.json` for each run (every step, max_memory_allocated and max_memory_reserved, host, GPU name), plus `summary.txt` and `runs.json`.
- The job aborts if the GPU name does not match the requested kind.

## Compare
`--compare` reports these quantities for each shape:
- whether the batches were identical on the two GPUs;
- the same-shape V100/L40S ratio (total time, and the median of per-batch ratios);
- the V100 per-real-frame time at the shape divided by the L40S per-real-frame time at (a);
- the projection, 3237 s times that last ratio.

For (a) the last ratio equals the same-shape ratio. For (b) it is my choice, because 3237 s was measured at (a).

## Expected runtime
- **L40S:** about 57 s per step at (a), so 14 steps take about 13 min. (b) takes about 7 min. Model load and batch build add about 1–2 min per run. Total about 25 min.
- **V100:** unknown; this is what the benchmark measures. Its time should be within the 2 h limit.

## Concerns and choices the dispatch left open
- **(a) will probably OOM on the V100.** The L40S peak was 34.3 GiB reserved, and the V100 has about 31.7 GiB, so the expandable-segments retry may also fail. In that case only (b) gives a V100 number, and the (a) projection is empty.
- **Seed:** the dispatch gave no value. I used 0, set in the `SEED` constant.
- **CPU threads:** the job has 8 CPUs, as the dispatch specified. The training ran with OMP_NUM_THREADS=16. I expect this to matter little, since the step was measured at 99.7 % compute on the GPU.
- **Cache manager:** it is off, so the same HDFs are read from /work. Data reading is not timed.
- **Model state during the run:** the model is updated for all 14 steps at the epoch-12 learning rate (1e-4). Only the benchmark's own copy changes.
